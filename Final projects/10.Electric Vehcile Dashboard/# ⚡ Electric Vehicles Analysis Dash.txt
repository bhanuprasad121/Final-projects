# ⚡ Electric Vehicles Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an **Electric Vehicles (EV) Analysis Dashboard** developed using **Power BI**. The dashboard provides comprehensive insights into electric vehicle adoption across Indian states, manufacturer performance, vehicle categories, monthly sales trends, and market growth. The analysis enables policymakers, manufacturers, and stakeholders to understand EV market dynamics and make informed, data-driven decisions.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze electric vehicle sales data to identify sales trends, state-wise EV adoption, manufacturer performance, vehicle category distribution, and market growth. The dashboard helps stakeholders monitor EV penetration, evaluate manufacturer competitiveness, identify high-growth markets, and support strategic planning for the expansion of electric mobility.

---

## 📂 Dataset

The project uses three datasets:

### 📅 dim_date
Contains calendar-related information including:
- Date

### 🚗 Electric Vehicle Sales by State
Contains:
- State
- Vehicle Category
- Electric Vehicles Sold
- Total Vehicles Sold
- EV Penetration (%)

### 🏭 Electric Vehicle Sales by Maker
Contains:
- Manufacturer

- Electric Vehicles Sold
- Total Vehicles Sold

# 📈 Key Insights

- Maharashtra records the highest electric vehicle sales among all states.
- Karnataka and Tamil Nadu are among the leading contributors to EV adoption.
- Two-wheelers dominate the Indian EV market compared to four-wheelers.
- OLA Electric is the leading EV manufacturer based on total sales.
- Several northeastern states show higher EV penetration despite lower sales volumes.
- Monthly EV sales fluctuate throughout the year, indicating seasonal demand.
- CAGR analysis highlights emerging states and manufacturers experiencing rapid growth.
- Market growth is driven by increasing consumer awareness and government initiatives.

---

# 💡 Business Recommendations

- Increase charging infrastructure in high-growth states.
- Expand dealership networks in states with rising EV adoption.
- Encourage manufacturers to invest in two-wheelers, which dominate the market.
- Promote government incentives in states with lower EV penetration.
- Focus marketing campaigns during peak sales months.
- Support emerging manufacturers with innovation and infrastructure partnerships.
- Continue monitoring CAGR to identify future high-growth markets.

# 📈 Conclusion

The Electric Vehicles Analysis Dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of India's EV market by analyzing sales performance, manufacturer contribution, state-wise adoption, vehicle categories, and growth trends. These insights support the continued growth of sustainable transportation and help accelerate India's transition toward electric mobility.
