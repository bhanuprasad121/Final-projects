## 📊 Dashboard Visualizations (Power BI)

The interactive Power BI dashboard was designed to analyze Dubai house prices, property types, neighborhood performance, and listing trends. The dashboard includes the following visualizations:

### 📌 KPI Cards
- Total Listings
- Average House Price
- Average Price per Square Foot

### 📊 Bar Chart
**Average Price by Neighborhood**
- Compares the average house price across Urban, Rural, and Suburb neighborhoods.

### 📊 Clustered Column Chart
**Total Listings by Bedrooms and Property Type**
- Displays the number of listings based on bedroom count (2–5 bedrooms) and property type (Apartment, Villa, Luxury House).

### 📊 Stacked Column Chart
**Total Listings by Neighborhood and Property Type**
- Shows the distribution of Apartments, Villas, and Luxury Houses across different neighborhoods.

### 📈 Line Chart
**Average Price by Year Built**
- Analyzes how the average property price changes based on the construction year.

### 📊 Stacked Bar Chart
**Sum of Price by Property Type and Price Bins**
- Compares the total property value across Apartments, Villas, and Luxury Houses within different price ranges.

### 🎛️ Slicers (Interactive Filters)
- Bathrooms
- Bedrooms
- Listing Category (Budget, Mid-Range, High-End)

These slicers allow users to dynamically filter the dashboard and analyze specific property segments.

---

## 📈 Dashboard Insights

- The dashboard contains approximately **2,000 property listings**.
- The average property price is approximately **223.71K**.
- The average price per square foot is around **113.12**.
- Villas contribute the highest total property value among all property types.
- Property prices remain relatively stable across neighborhoods with slight variations.
- Houses with **4 and 5 bedrooms** have a higher number of listings.
- Urban, Rural, and Suburb neighborhoods have a balanced distribution of property listings.
- Newer and older properties exhibit moderate variations in average pricing.
- Mid-range properties dominate the market, while luxury properties contribute significantly to the total market value.
- Interactive filters allow users to analyze pricing trends based on bedrooms, bathrooms, and listing categories.
