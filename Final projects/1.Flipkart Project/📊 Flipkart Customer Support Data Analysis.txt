📊 Flipkart Customer Support Data Analysis (EDA)
📌 Project Overview

This project performs Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on Flipkart Customer Support data to understand customer complaints, customer satisfaction (CSAT), agent performance, and product-related support issues. The objective is to identify meaningful business insights that can help improve customer service and enhance the overall customer experience.

🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze customer support interactions and identify the factors affecting customer satisfaction. The analysis helps understand complaint patterns, agent performance, product category issues, and regional trends to support better business decisions.

🔍 Key Insights
Electronics and Lifestyle categories received the highest number of customer complaints.
Hyderabad and New Delhi recorded the highest complaint volumes.
Customer satisfaction remained consistent across different agent shifts and tenure groups.
Product price showed very little correlation with customer satisfaction.
Connected handling time also had minimal influence on CSAT scores.
GiftCard category showed comparatively lower customer satisfaction and may require further investigation.
💡 Business Recommendations
Improve support quality for Electronics and Lifestyle product categories.
Allocate more customer support resources to high-complaint cities.
Investigate recurring issues related to GiftCard services.
Continue maintaining consistent training programs across all agent shifts.
Focus on improving issue resolution quality rather than product pricing.
📈 Conclusion

The analysis provides valuable insights into Flipkart's customer support operations. Most customers reported high satisfaction regardless of product price or agent tenure, indicating that customer experience is driven primarily by service quality and issue resolution. The findings can help Flipkart optimize customer support processes, improve operational efficiency, and enhance customer satisfaction.
