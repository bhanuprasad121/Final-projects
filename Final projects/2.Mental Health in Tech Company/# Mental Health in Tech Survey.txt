# Mental Health in Tech Survey – Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

## Project Overview

This project performs **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** on the **Mental Health in Tech Survey** dataset to understand the factors affecting employees' mental well-being in the technology industry. The analysis focuses on workplace support, treatment-seeking behavior, company policies, and employee perceptions regarding mental health.

## Business Objective

The objective of this project is to identify the key factors influencing employee mental health and evaluate how workplace policies, benefits, and organizational support impact treatment and overall well-being. The insights can help organizations build healthier workplaces and improve employee productivity.

## Dataset Information

The dataset contains employee survey responses with features such as:

* Age
* Gender
* Country
* State
* Family History
* Treatment
* Work Interference
* Number of Employees
* Remote Work
* Tech Company
* Mental Health Benefits
* Care Options
* Wellness Program
* Seek Help
* Anonymity
* Leave Policy
* Mental Health Consequences
* Physical Health Consequences
* Coworkers
* Supervisor
* Mental Health Interview
* Physical Health Interview
* Mental vs Physical Health
* Observed Consequences

## Project Workflow

1. Imported the dataset into Python.
2. Cleaned the data by removing unnecessary columns and handling missing values.
3. Removed duplicate records and corrected data inconsistencies.
4. Performed Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA).
5. Created visualizations to identify trends and relationships.
6. Generated business insights and recommendations.

## Key Insights

* Employees with a family history of mental illness are more likely to seek treatment.
* Work interference has a strong relationship with mental health treatment.
* Many employees are unaware of their company's mental health policies.
* Tech companies generally provide better mental health support than non-tech companies.
* Remote workers show a slightly more positive perception of workplace mental health support.
* Wellness programs and care options encourage employees to seek help.

## Business Recommendations

* Increase awareness of mental health policies and available support.
* Provide employee counseling and wellness programs.
* Encourage open discussions between employees and managers.
* Reduce stigma around mental health through awareness campaigns.
* Ensure mental health receives equal importance as physical health.
* Implement early intervention programs before mental health affects work performance.

## Conclusion

The analysis highlights the importance of workplace mental health initiatives and organizational support. By improving awareness, providing accessible mental health resources, and encouraging open communication, organizations can improve employee well-being, increase productivity, reduce absenteeism, and create a healthier work environment.
